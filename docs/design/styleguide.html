Motion

  • Only two things move on their own: the cursor blink and the counter pop.
  • Counter pop: on in the landing, off by default in the editor, user toggle.
  • Reduced motion always wins: no blink, no pop, and the toggle is disabled with the reason shown. Never overridable.
  • Everything else moves only in answer to the user: open, close, confirm.
